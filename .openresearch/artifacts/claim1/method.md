# Method

The verifier encodes the four external quartic signatures and applies the neural-channel equality partition plus propagator color-pairing rule to the nontrivial `F` target. It must emit exactly one direct Gaussian-propagator term and one term containing an internal `F` quartic vertex with two propagators. A separately written checker audits the serialized topology and algebraic structure. The unpaired-color candidate is the negative selection-rule control.
