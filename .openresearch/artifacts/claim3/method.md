# Method

Positive homogeneity on the real line reduces every admissible activation to slopes `a_plus` and `a_minus` on the two half-lines. The verifier computes the exact Gaussian expectations `E[sigma(Z)^2]=A K` and `E[sigma'(Z)^2]=A`, proves all non-propagating diagram sources vanish under differentiation with respect to `K`, and emits the induction schema for every correction order. Independent Gauss-Hermite quadrature checks ReLU, LeakyReLU (`alpha=0.1`) and identity at three variances. GeLU is the intended negative control because its derivative-squared expectation varies with `K`.

The exact-scale empirical verifier samples five million iid Gaussian parameter sets for each of widths 20 and 80. Each initialization is evaluated with both ReLU and LeakyReLU using common random numbers. It estimates the paper's two-output trace-averaged diagonal and off-diagonal NTK with two unbiased Rademacher parameter-space probes. It retains 100 raw block means of 50,000 networks for each activation-width pair. The exact infinite-width references come from a closed-form arc-cosine recursion, independently checked on the diagonal against `L r^(L-1) mean(x^2)`. A tiny-network exact Jacobian calibrates the stochastic trace estimator. The diagonal uses a precommitted 99% equivalence interval with a one-percent relative margin; the off-diagonal must show the expected correction and serves as a within-architecture control.
