# Limitations and deviations

The proof is for the exact paper assumptions and finite depth. It says nothing about biases, non-Gaussian initialization, convolutional layers, off-diagonal NTKs, individual finite samples, or infinite-depth/width joint limits. The Monte Carlo experiment uses two source-range widths rather than digitizing every plotted width. Its two-probe Hutchinson trace is unbiased but adds estimator variance relative to the source implementation's exact recursive Jacobian trace; the independent checker measures that deviation. The one-percent equivalence margin is finite-precision corroboration, not a proof of exact equality; the symbolic certificate carries the universal theorem.
